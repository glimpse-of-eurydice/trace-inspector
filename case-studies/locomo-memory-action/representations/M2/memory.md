# Memory: stable user profile

## Core profile

Andrew is strongly restored by nature and outdoor activity. Hiking is a
particularly meaningful way for him to find peace and reset when life becomes
chaotic. He enjoys challenging outdoor experiences: a successful
rock-climbing class left him eager to do more, with interest in kayaking and
bungee jumping. `[F01, F02]`

Andrew values an active but balanced lifestyle. Work demands sometimes keep
him indoors and make it harder to maintain outdoor routines, so plans should
respect his work-life balance. When he needs something easier, he appreciates
simple restorative activities such as morning coffee, a lunchtime walk, time
at a cozy cafe, or bringing plants into his home. `[F03, F04, F06, F07, F08]`

## Personal context

Andrew has a young dog named Toby and would like to include him in outdoor
activities. Pet-friendly options are relevant, although Toby's age has
previously made hiking more difficult. `[F05]`

Andrew is willing to make concrete outdoor plans even when work has recently
limited his access to nature. His profile should therefore not be reduced to
either “always choose an ambitious adventure” or “avoid outdoor activity.”
`[F06, F10]`

## Scheduling preferences and commitments

- Favor plans that combine restoration, nature, and manageable effort.
- Use lower-burden activities when a demanding outdoor plan would conflict
  with work-life balance.
